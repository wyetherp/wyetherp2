Let's Flash Your ESP32

Step 1: Plug In Your ESP32

Connect your ESP32 to your computer with the USB cable.

Step 2: Click the Button Below

Your browser will ask you to select a device. Choose the one that says "USB" or "Serial" or "CP210x" or "CH340".

Step 3: Wait (~30 seconds)

Don't unplug anything! The code is uploading.

Step 4: Look at Your ESP32

When done, look at your ESP32. There's a small LED on the board. Is it breathing?

If yes: YOU DID IT! 🎉

Troubleshooting

Most problems are one of these. Try them in order:

  • 1. No device appears when I click FLASH

    Try a different USB cable. Most cables are "charge-only" and can't transfer data. iPhone cables work.

  • 2. Device appears but flash fails

    Try a different USB port. Some ports don't have enough power. Also try unplugging the ESP32, waiting 5 seconds, and plugging it back in.

  • 3. Flash completes but LED doesn't breathe

    Unplug the ESP32 and plug it back in. Some ESP32s need a restart after first flash.

  • 4. I'm on Mac and it's not working

    You might need CH340 drivers. Google "CH340 driver Mac" and install it. Then restart and try again.

  • 5. I'm on Windows and it's not working

    You might need CP210x drivers. Go to Silicon Labs and download the driver.
