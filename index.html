Troubleshooting

Try these in order. Most problems are simple.

  • No device appears

    Try a different USB cable. Most are charge-only and can't transfer data.

  • Device appears but flash fails

    Try a different USB port. Unplug, wait 5 seconds, plug back in.

  • Flash completes but LED doesn't breathe

    Unplug and replug the ESP32. Some need a restart after first flash.

  • Mac not working

    You may need CH340 drivers. Search "CH340 driver Mac" and install.

  • Windows not working

    You may need CP210x drivers from Silicon Labs.

  • Still stuck

    Email wja326@lehigh.edu with your computer type, browser, and the error message.

What Is CRUMB?

The Idea

CRUMB teaches electronics through doing, not reading. Genesis Zero proves a truth most people never discover: if you can click a button, you can program a computer. The barriers were never real.

The Pattern

Every computer follows the same pattern: Input → Process → Output. Your phone. Your car. This ESP32 chip. Once you see it, you can build anything.

The Path

  • Genesis Zero: Control the onboard LED — 5 minutes
  • Genesis One: Wire an external LED — 15 minutes
  • Genesis Two: Add sensors — 30 minutes
  • Genesis Three: Displays & buttons — 1 hour
  • Genesis ∞: Build whatever you imagine
