Troubleshooting

  • No device appears

    Try a different USB cable—most are charge-only.

  • Flash fails

    Try a different USB port. Unplug, wait 5s, replug.

  • LED doesn't breathe

    Unplug and replug the ESP32.

  • Mac issues

    Search "CH340 driver Mac" and install.

  • Windows issues

    Get CP210x drivers from Silicon Labs.

  • Still stuck

    Email wja326@lehigh.edu
