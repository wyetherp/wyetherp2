Let's Fix It

No device found

Try a different USB cable — most only charge, can't transfer data.

Flash fails

Try a different USB port. Unplug, wait 5 sec, replug.

LED doesn't breathe

Unplug and replug the ESP32. Try flashing again.

What Is This?

The Idea

CRUMB teaches electronics by doing, not by reading. You build first, understand later. Genesis Zero proves you can program a computer in 15 minutes. The barriers were never real.

The Philosophy

All computers are the same. Your phone, your laptop, a rocket — they all run instructions that move electricity. Learn one, understand all. Complexity is just more instructions.

Why It Exists

Built for my brother Luke, who struggles with technology not designed for how his brain works. If we're going to build a future full of computers, people deserve to understand them.

Open Source

GPL-3.0. Use it, modify it, share it, build on it. No gatekeeping. No subscriptions. Yours forever.
